# Great Zone — Women's Clothing Website

A luxurious, high-fashion, trending static website for **Great Zone (Women's Clothing)**, inspired by the brand logo aesthetic: **Royal Navy Blue**, **Metallic Gold**, and **Champagne Accents**.

> **Tagline:** STYLE • COMFORT • YOU

---

## 🌟 Key Features

1. **Logo & Brand Identity Matching:**
   - Deep Royal Midnight Navy (`#040D1A`, `#08172E`) and Regal Metallic Gold gradients (`#FBE8A6` to `#D4AF37`).
   - Integrated brand logo in navigation bar, hero badge, and footer.

2. **Runway Hero Section:**
   - Cinematic hero banner with high-impact fashion imagery.
   - Live stats ("50K+ Happy Women", "100% Pure Silk", "4.9 Customer Rating").
   - Action buttons: *Explore Collection* & *View Lookbook*.

3. **Trending Categories Grid:**
   - Royal Silk Sarees
   - Festive Lehengas
   - Couture Evening Gowns
   - Chic Power Co-ords

4. **Interactive Products Showcase:**
   - Filter tabs: *All Ensembles*, *Trending Now*, *Festive Glam*, *Western Chic*.
   - **Quick View Modal** with size selector (XS, S, M, L, XL), fabric details, and direct cart/WhatsApp order.
   - Dynamic Wishlist toggle with badge counter.

5. **Functional Slide-out Shopping Bag (Cart Drawer):**
   - Live item quantity counter and subtotal calculation.
   - **Free Shipping Progress Tracker** (Target: ₹1,999).
   - Promo coupon code system (use code: `GREATZONE10` for 10% off).
   - **WhatsApp One-Click Order:** Generates a pre-formatted message with items, sizes, and total for direct checkout.

6. **Editorial Lookbook ("The Midnight & Gold Affair"):**
   - High-fashion magazine-style editorial layout highlighting fabric quality, zari craftsmanship, and all-day comfort.

7. **Social Proof & Community:**
   - Customer reviews from verified buyers.
   - Instagram `#GreatZoneWomen` community photo wall.
   - VIP Royal Circle Newsletter with instant discount voucher.

8. **Direct WhatsApp Integration:**
   - Floating WhatsApp button on the bottom-right for instant customer inquiries.
   - WhatsApp order buttons on every product card and cart drawer.
